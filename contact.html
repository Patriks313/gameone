Contact

Feedback, ideas and questions are genuinely welcome

Shadow Council is made and run by one person, Patrik Stalbarg, and it gets better mostly because players write in with things they noticed. Whether you've found a bug, thought of a word pair that belongs in the game, or have a question about how something works, here is where to reach me.

Suggesting a word pair

The word pairs are the heart of the game, and the list grows over time. A good pair is two words that are clearly related but not identical — close enough that an Evil player can steer toward one while listening for the other, but not so obvious that the link gives the game away. If you have one you think fits, in English or Swedish, send it to hello@greblats.com with a line about why it works.

Reporting a problem

If something went wrong mid-game, the most useful things to include are what phone and browser you were using, roughly how many players were in the room, and what you saw happen. Even a rough description helps track down the cause. Most "frozen game" reports turn out to be a phone briefly losing its connection — the FAQ covers how to avoid that on long sessions.

Response times

This is a small project run by one person alongside other work, so replies aren't instant — but every message is read, and most get an answer within a few days.
